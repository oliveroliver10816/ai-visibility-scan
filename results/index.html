Where the buyer actually gets sent

This is the finding with the most direct revenue consequence, and it is separate from the sentiment problem.

0 ozem-plus.store — our site, the one with the six organic sales — was cited zero times in sixty answers.
3 ozem-plus.com is cited instead, and named as "the official website" by both engines.
2 buyopluscaps.com — cited as the checkout the order button leads to.
5 shop-apotheke.com — offered as a purchase route, via marketplace seller WR-Products.

Asked point-blank where to buy, the engines route to ozem-plus.com, buyopluscaps.com, Shop-Apotheke, Kaufland and eBay. Those are the merchant's own official site and funnel, plus retailers. They are citing correctly — we are the affiliate, not the brand, so our site was never going to be named "the official website".

The cost is the tag, not the traffic. When an engine sends a buyer straight to the merchant, the sale completes without our sub1 on it. The purchase still happens. We just don't get paid for it. Every one of our six sales came from an organic click through our own /go/ link — the only path that carries our tag.

There is no fix for this on our side. We cannot outrank the brand's own domain for its own brand name, and we should not try to look like it.

What we do next

The honest read is that this property is finished as an AI-visibility play. What survives is the instrument and one transferable finding.

  1. Take Ozem+ AI visibility off the desk

    Three separate reasons, any one of which is enough. The category answers have no brand slot to win. The brand questions are answered with warnings we cannot argue away, because the Verbraucherzentrale listing is real. And the buy-intent answers correctly point at the merchant's own domain, which we cannot and should not displace. This is not a work-harder problem.

  2. Keep the instrument, point it somewhere it can win

    The sheet, the collector and the extraction ran end to end in a day for zero spend, and answered the question cleanly. Run the identical thing on a property that is not on a consumer-warning list — that tells us whether the zero is this brand or the whole category, and it is the only way to find out.

  3. Bank the one thing that transfers

    The engines here quote regulators, marketplaces and review platforms, and no affiliate review site at all. That is a finding about what these engines will quote, and it applies to every site we run. Worth carrying into the next build rather than re-learning.
