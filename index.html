What do ChatGPT and Gemini say about these products?

You will ask 30 questions in two AI tools and write down what each one answers. That is the whole job. You do not need to understand the products, judge the answers, or change anything — just record exactly what you see.

  1. Open ChatGPT in one tab and Gemini in another. Both free accounts are fine.
  2. Copy the question below with the Copy button and paste it into whichever tool the page is asking for.
  3. Read the answer and fill in the three boxes on this page.
  4. Press Save and next. Your work is stored in this browser, so you can stop and come back.
  5. When the bar reaches 100%, press Download and send the file back.
Two rules that matter more than speed. Write only what you actually see on screen — never guess, never tidy up, never fill a box from memory of an earlier answer. And if the tool shows no sources or links at all, tick the “no sources shown” box rather than leaving it blank. An empty box and a genuine “nothing shown” mean different things to us.
